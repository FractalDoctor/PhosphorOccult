PHOSPHOROCCULT

Stellar Occultation Predictor

Predict when the Moon, planets, and asteroids pass in front of stars. For your exact location. Computed entirely on your device.

PhosphorOccult is a precision occultation prediction tool for amateur astronomers, IOTA observers, and serious stargazers. All calculations use established astronomical algorithms and a catalogue of nearly 9,000 stars from ESA Hipparcos.

PREDICTIONS

  • Lunar occultations — Moon occulting stars and planets with sub-second contact times
  • Planetary occultations — rare events where a planet occults a star
  • Asteroid occultations — eight major asteroids tracked as occulters
  • All predictions topocentric — corrected for your exact position and elevation

INSTRUMENTS

  • Chronological event list with contact times, position angles, limb classification
  • Polar sky plot — zenithal projection of tonight’s sky with body tracks
  • Telescope viewfinder — animated event simulation with time scrubber
  • Instrument readout panel — full immersion/emersion data for each event

COMPUTATION

  • Meeus Ch.47 lunar ephemeris (ELP2000-82 series)
  • VSOP87 truncated planetary positions
  • Keplerian asteroid orbital elements
  • 8,870 stars from ESA Hipparcos (public domain)
  • Prediction window up to 5 years
  • Background refresh keeps predictions current overnight

No internet required. No ads. No tracking. No subscription. Part of the Phosphor Suite.
