PHOSPHOROCCULT — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: March 2026

DATA COLLECTION

PhosphorOccult does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

NETWORK REQUESTS

PhosphorOccult does not make any network requests. All astronomical calculations are performed entirely on-device using bundled ephemeris data and star catalogues. The app functions completely offline.

LOCATION DATA

PhosphorOccult requests your location solely to compute topocentric occultation predictions for your position. Your coordinates are used locally on your device and are never transmitted, stored externally, or shared with any third party. You may also enter coordinates manually without granting location access.

LOCAL DATA STORAGE

PhosphorOccult stores your configuration (observer location, prediction window, selected occulting bodies) and cached prediction results on your device using standard iOS storage. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to any external service.

ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

PhosphorOccult does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

PhosphorOccult does not communicate with any third-party services.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

PhosphorOccult does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

CONTACT

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the PhosphorOccult GitHub repository.
